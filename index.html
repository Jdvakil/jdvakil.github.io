    I work on robot learning, mobile manipulation, and embodied AI, building intelligent agents that interact robustly with the physical world.

    I'm a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Alessandro Roncone. Before my PhD, I was a Robotics Research Engineer at Meta AI (FAIR), where I contributed to large-scale initiatives such as RT-X, RoboAgent, and OK-Robot.
